About Me

I am a PhD student in the Electrical Engineering Department at Tel Aviv University, working in machine learning theory and generalization. My current research focuses on theoretical guarantees for learning algorithms and structural insights into generalization phenomena.

I am co-advised by Roi Livni (TAU) and Shay Moran (Technion). My interests span learning theory, generalization bounds, and algorithmic structure in high-dimensional models.
